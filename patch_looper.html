Using the Phrase Looper


Turn up the FX1 send on the track(s) you want to loop, open FX1 and choose Phrase Looper (where Granular was). The FX1 title shows the state: Looper, ARMED, REC 1/2, PLAY, DUB or STOP.




RECEmpty: record. At the end of the bars the loop plays by itself. Recording: close the loop now. Playing: overdub on / off. Stopped: play.


STOPStop. Press again to play from the start. Stopping during a take keeps it.


CLEAREmpty the loop.


SyncOn: with the sequencer stopped, REC waits and recording starts when you press start.


Bars1 or 2 bars at the project BPM.


LevelLoop volume. The FX1 return carries only the loop.


DubHow loud new playing is layered while overdubbing.




REC, STOP and CLEAR act once per press and never stay lit. While the loop plays, the tracks feeding FX1 are muted automatically, so you hear only the loop. They are unmuted while you record or overdub, and when you stop, clear or leave the looper. Tracks you muted yourself are never touched.


Loops are stored as 16-bit audio. The TOOLS page shows the longest loop, for example Cblt L4.0s: 4 seconds is 2 bars at 120 BPM or faster. When 2 bars don’t fit, the loop is 1 bar and the title shows REC 1/1. Switching FX1 to another mode empties the loop.
